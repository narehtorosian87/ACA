Hospitality Weekly Market Analysis

A weekly research briefing for a Hotelchamp Product Manager — the top hospitality & travel-tech updates from the last 7 days, translated into what they could mean for the booking engine roadmap.

What it does

Every run, the skill searches a fixed list of credible trade outlets and official brand/OTA newsrooms, filters to the last 7 days, and drops opinion pieces in favor of reported news. It picks up to 10 of the most significant updates, split across two focus areas:

General hospitality industry Hospitality tech & AI

For each item it writes a short factual summary, a specific "what this could mean for Hotelchamp / the booking engine" analysis, and a cited source link — then appends the week as a new dated section to the running log, newest first, skipping anything already covered in a prior week.

Approved sources

Trade press
  • Skift
  • Hotel Tech Report
  • PhocusWire
  • Hospitality Net
  • Travel Weekly
  • Hotel News Now
Official newsrooms
  • Booking.com / Booking Holdings
  • Expedia Group
  • Airbnb
  • Marriott, Hilton, Accor, IHG, Hyatt

Where the output lives

Each week's report is appended to products/hospitality-weekly-market-analysis/weekly-log.md in this repo — one running file, most recent week at the top, used at each quarter's planning cycle to spot recurring themes and roadmap signals.

Running it

Installed as a global Claude Code skill at ~/.claude/skills/hospitality-weekly-market-analysis/, and mirrored in this repo at .claude/skills/hospitality-weekly-market-analysis/. It's scheduled to run automatically every Monday morning (8am Yerevan time), and can also be triggered manually:

Ask Claude Code: "run the hospitality weekly market analysis"
