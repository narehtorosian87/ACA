# PM interview question bank

Standard categories to draw from in step 4 of `SKILL.md`. Always adapt these to the specific job post and research findings rather than using them verbatim — the examples below are starting points, not a script.

## Behavioral / leadership
- Tell me about a time you disagreed with a stakeholder (eng lead, designer, exec) — what happened?
- Describe a project that failed or missed its goal. What did you do differently after?
- Tell me about a time you had to influence without authority.
- How have you handled conflicting priorities from different stakeholders?
- Describe a time you had to make a decision with incomplete data.
- Tell me about a time you killed a feature or project you had championed.

## Product sense / design
- Design a product/feature for [underserved user group].
- How would you improve [this company's actual product]?
- Walk me through a product you love and one you'd redesign, and why.
- A key metric drops 20% — how do you approach it? (borderline analytical, often asked as product sense)

## Execution / analytical
- How would you define success metrics for [feature/product]?
- Walk me through how you'd prioritize a roadmap given [limited eng capacity / conflicting stakeholder asks].
- Size the market for [product/feature].
- A/B test came back with no significant result — what do you do?

## Strategy / vision
- Where should this product be in 3 years?
- Should [this company] enter [adjacent market]? Build vs. buy vs. partner?
- What's the biggest threat to this company's product in the next 2 years?

## Culture / motivation
- Why this company? Why this role? Why PM?
- What's a product decision you'd have made differently than this company did?
- What questions do you have for us? (see SKILL.md step 7 — prep the user's own questions here, not just answers)

## Technical/domain fluency (only if the JD signals it)
- Comfort with SQL/data pulls, APIs, or the specific technical domain (e.g. payments, ML, infra)
- How they've worked with engineers on technical tradeoffs
