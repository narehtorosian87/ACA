# Research areas

Use this as the checklist for step 3 of `SKILL.md`. For each area, prefer primary sources (company site, official newsroom, the product itself, exec's own words) over secondhand summaries.

## 1. Company & product
- What the company actually sells and to whom (business model: B2B/B2C, subscription/transaction/ads, etc.)
- Company stage: bootstrapped, VC-backed (which round, how much, when), public (recent earnings highlights)
- Size signals: headcount, offices, growth trajectory

## 2. The specific product area this role owns
- What the product does today, concretely — not just the marketing page
- Recent releases/changelog if public
- User sentiment: G2/Capterra reviews, App Store/Play Store reviews, relevant subreddits or forums, HN/Twitter mentions
- Anything publicly said about roadmap direction (blog posts, job postings for other roles on the same team, exec interviews)

## 3. Market & competitors
- 2-4 direct competitors and how this company differentiates
- Where this company is winning/losing in the market narrative (analyst pieces, comparison articles)

## 4. Recent news (last 6-12 months)
- Funding rounds, acquisitions, layoffs, leadership changes
- Founder/exec interviews or podcast appearances — these often preview what they care about in interviews
- Confirm actual publish dates before citing anything as "recent"

## 5. Team & role context
- Why this role is likely open: new team/growth vs. backfill (tenure of predecessor if discoverable via LinkedIn)
- Reporting line and team size if the posting or LinkedIn reveals it
- Any named interviewers — their background, what they've written/spoken about publicly

## 6. Interview process & culture signals
- Glassdoor/Blind interview experience reports for this company (and ideally this role) if findable
- Stated company values/principles (careers page, blog)
- Engineering/product blog tone — reveals how they think about building
