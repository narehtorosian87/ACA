# Approved sources

Use only these. If a story only shows up on a source not listed here, skip it rather than substituting a less credible outlet.

## Credible trade press (primary sources for both focus areas)
- **Skift** — skift.com — industry news, market data, trends
- **Hotel Tech Report** — hoteltechreport.com — hospitality tech/vendor news and reviews
- **PhocusWire** — phocuswire.com — travel tech, distribution, OTA news
- **Hospitality Net** — hospitalitynet.org — general hospitality industry news and press releases
- **Travel Weekly** — travelweekly.com — travel/hospitality industry news
- **Hotel News Now (STR/CoStar)** — hotelnewsnow.com — hotel performance data and market news
- **Skift's sub-brands** (Skift Travel Tech, Skift Meetings) also count as Skift

## Official brand & OTA newsrooms (primary sources, always credible for their own news)
- Booking.com / Booking Holdings — news.booking.com, press releases, investor releases
- Expedia Group — press.expediagroup.com
- Airbnb — news.airbnb.com
- Trip.com Group — investor/press releases
- Marriott International — news.marriott.com
- Hilton — stories.hilton.com, investor releases
- Accor — press.accor.com
- IHG Hotels & Resorts — ihgplc.com/en/news-and-media
- Hyatt — newsroom.hyatt.com

## What does NOT count
- Opinion columns, "thought leadership" contributor posts, unverified aggregator blogs, LinkedIn posts, generic SEO listicles, and any outlet not on this list. If in doubt, prefer skipping the story over stretching the source list.

## Search tips
- Search each outlet's site directly when possible (e.g. `site:skift.com AI booking` or `site:hoteltechreport.com`) rather than relying on generic queries, to stay inside the approved list.
- For brand/OTA newsroom items, go straight to the press/investor relations page — general news search often misses these.
