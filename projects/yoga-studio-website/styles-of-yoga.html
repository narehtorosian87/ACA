Styles of Yoga

Which class is actually for you

No two styles ask the same thing of your body. Here's the short version of each one we teach.

Hatha Yoga

Foundational

A slower practice that holds each posture for several breaths, with time to check alignment. Good for beginners or anyone who wants to work on the basics.

Vinyasa Flow

Dynamic

Movement linked to breath, one pose flowing into the next without much pause. Builds heat and strength, suited to a more active practice.

Ashtanga

Structured

A set sequence performed in the same order every time. Physically demanding, and best for students who like consistency and a real challenge.

Yin Yoga

Slow & still

Postures held for three to five minutes, targeting the connective tissue rather than the muscles. Good for flexibility and for quieting a busy mind.

Restorative Yoga

Recovery

Fully supported postures using bolsters and blocks, held for long periods. About rest and nervous system recovery, not strength or stretch.

Prenatal Yoga

Gentle

Modified postures that are safe throughout pregnancy, with a focus on breath and gentle strength. Suited to expecting students at any stage, with guidance on modifications.

Sthira Sukham Asanam

A posture should be steady and comfortable. Whichever style you choose, that's really the only rule.

Not sure where to start?

Hatha and Gentle Hatha classes are the easiest place to begin. From there, most students settle into Vinyasa, Yin, or a mix of both.
