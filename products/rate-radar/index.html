Methodology. Rates are 2-adult, 1-night live rates pulled from trivago's metasearch, shown in EUR, for the four snapshot dates in the date picker — pick one to see that day's market. Competitor sets are up to 8 nearby properties closest in price to the subject hotel, matching its city and star rating via radius search around each hotel's coordinates; sister properties under the same ownership group are excluded. "Position" compares the subject rate to the median of that competitor set, which is more resistant to outliers than a plain average — this matters most for wide 5-star markets (Milan, London, Amsterdam) that span boutique design hotels through ultra-luxury flagships. Aethos Milan's Fri 25 Sep snapshot shows an unusually high rate across the whole Milan market that date — read as a live demand spike, not a data error.

Known gaps. Aethos Monterosa (Ayas, Aosta Valley) is a remote ski-resort property with thin Trivago coverage — it has no bookable rate for Thu 15 Oct or Fri 30 Oct, and no comparable competitor set was found nearby for any date. Bad Ischl (Grand Elisabeth's market) is a small spa town, so a few of its competitors don't return a live rate on every snapshot date — this is normal thin-market coverage, not a broken listing.

Date picker. Live rate pulls are point-in-time, so the picker is limited to the four dates already fetched rather than any arbitrary day. Ask for a different date and it can be added as a new snapshot.
