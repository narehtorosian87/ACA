Methodology. Rates are 2-adult, 1-night rates from trivago's metasearch, shown in EUR. Competitor sets are up to 8 nearby properties closest in price to the subject hotel, matching its city and star rating via radius search around each hotel's coordinates; sister properties under the same ownership group are excluded. "Position" compares the subject rate to the median of that competitor set, which is more resistant to outliers than a plain average — this matters most for wide 5-star markets (Milan, London, Amsterdam) that span boutique design hotels through ultra-luxury flagships.

Refresh. A scheduled job pulls fresh rates for all 7 hotels once a day and republishes this page automatically — no action needed. The date picker marks every date that currently has data with a dot.

Known gaps. Aethos Monterosa (Ayas, Aosta Valley) is a remote ski-resort property with thin Trivago coverage, and no comparable competitor set has been found nearby on any date fetched so far. Bad Ischl (Grand Elisabeth's market) is a small spa town, so a few of its competitors don't return a live rate on every date — this is normal thin-market coverage, not a broken listing.
